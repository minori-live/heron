# Elevation

Hairline edges and recessed fills establish most hierarchy. Shadows are long and soft, as on the
product website, and are reserved for content that moves above the normal layout: a raised
segment or selected row (`raised`), cards (`sm`, `md`), and menus, popovers, and modal dialogs
(`lg`). Floating chrome is translucent glass with `--ui-backdrop-glass`. Numeric z-index values
only exist in tokens.
